## Launch note for Insights

Do not publish an empty Insights archive. The route may be withheld from navigation until at least three substantial pieces are ready.

## Emotional navigation rule

The seven persona names remain internal.

Public pages should use:

- Recognizable situations
- Emotional validation
- Protected values
- Desired movement
- Relevant proof
- Clear service paths

The homepage will present three primary recognition choices. Audience and service pages will provide deeper modulation for the internal archetypes.

## Header

Include:

- oobCREATIVE wordmark or logo
- Who We Help
- Services
- Work
- About
- Insights when launched
- Start a Conversation action
- Accessible mobile navigation

## Footer

Include:

- Positioning statement
- Primary navigation
- Audience links
- Service links
- Direct email
- Privacy
- Copyright
- Roaring Fork Valley, Colorado location statement if approved

## URL rules

- Lowercase
- Hyphenated words
- Descriptive service language
- Stable after launch
- Redirect before changing any live URL
- No persona names in URLs

10. Privacy and 404
