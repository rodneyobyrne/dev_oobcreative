# Website Content Architecture

## Global objective

The website should help a qualified visitor answer:

1. Does oobCREATIVE understand the situation I am carrying?
2. Is there a relevant and bounded path?
3. Has Rodney completed work that demonstrates this capability?
4. What will the process ask of me?
5. What should I do next?

## Homepage

### 1. Hero

Use the approved hero from `messaging-architecture.md`.

Emotional function:

- Establish competence
- Avoid overwhelming detail
- Signal human-led technology
- Create a clear route to proof and services

Visual function:

- One unified oobCREATIVE visual world
- Human and environmental detail rather than generic office imagery
- Layered composition suggesting connected disciplines
- No robot, handshake, or corporate-team clichés

### 2. Recognition

Heading:

> What has outgrown the way you are working?

Present three primary choices:

1. Clarify the work
2. Build a system around it
3. Move an important initiative forward

Each choice links to relevant services and proof.

### 3. Featured work

Lead with:

- Building the Backline — Rock and Roll Academy Aspen
- Everett Health Partners
- Recovery Looks Different — Varetto Recovery

Add Mind Springs as a fourth proof item or a supporting healthcare feature.

Each card includes:

- Starting condition
- Strategic insight
- Connected work
- Verified outcome stage

### 4. Services

Present three primary service families.

Each service card begins with the recognizable situation, not a deliverable list.

### 5. Who we help

Present three public markets:

- Behavioral health and human services
- Nonprofits and community organizations
- Expert-led firms and consultants

Each links to one audience page and one proof example.

### 6. How the work moves

Use:

- Understand
- Build
- Equip

Explain Guided, Co-Created, and Full Implementation below the stages.

### 7. About preview

Introduce Rodney's cross-disciplinary experience as the reason strategy and execution can stay connected.

### 8. Final action

Use the approved final CTA.

## Who We Help parent page

Purpose:

Help visitors recognize sector-specific pressures without creating a separate page for each named persona.

Structure:

1. Introductory position
2. Three audience groups
3. Common emotional conditions
4. Relevant services
5. Relevant case studies
6. Fit and qualification notes
7. Start a Conversation

## Behavioral Health and Human Services

### Opening

> Behavioral-health organizations carry clinical responsibility, operational complexity, public trust, and deeply personal human stories. Their communication systems need to make that work understandable without making it simplistic.

### Situations

- The public does not understand the model of care
- Programs are difficult to distinguish
- Admissions information creates uncertainty
- Leadership expertise is not visible
- Staff communication is fragmented
- Growth requires a clearer payer, donor, or referral story
- AI is being adopted without appropriate boundaries

### Proof

- Varetto Recovery
- Everett Health Partners
- Mind Springs Health and West Springs Hospital
- Aspen Strong

## Nonprofits and Community Organizations

### Opening

> A strong mission does not automatically produce a clear public story, sustainable fundraising system, or manageable communications workload.

### Situations

- The mission depends too heavily on the executive director
- Grants repeatedly require the same information
- Programs have outgrown the original identity
- Events are disconnected from fundraising and recruitment
- Sponsors do not understand their impact
- The website does not support the next stage of growth

### Proof

- Rock and Roll Academy Aspen
- Aspen Strong
- Community mental-health campaigns

## Expert-Led Firms and Consultants

### Opening

> Expertise can be extensive and still be difficult for a buyer to understand, compare, or act on.

### Situations

- The team appears as separate résumés
- Services are broad or overlapping
- Buyers do not recognize which expertise applies to them
- The website describes capabilities but does not guide decisions
- Valuable knowledge could become assessments, tools, or products
- The founder repeatedly explains the same distinctions

### Proof

- Everett Health Partners
- PayerKey product concept
- Enterprise and agency experience

## Services parent page

Structure:

1. Emotional introduction
2. Three primary services
3. Project Recovery Assessment
4. Participation modes
5. Proof matched to each service
6. Investment and timeline guidance when approved
7. Start a Conversation

## Individual service page standard

1. Searchable H1
2. Recognizable situation
3. Validation and protected value
4. What changes
5. What may be included
6. Early visible win
7. Completion evidence
8. Participation options
9. Relevant proof
10. Fit and non-fit
11. Timeline and investment guidance
12. Next step

## Work page

Use filters sparingly. Initial categories:

- Behavioral Health
- Mission and Fundraising
- Expert Positioning
- Communication Systems
- Campaigns and Launches

Every case study must label its outcome stage:

- Completed deliverables
- Strategic readiness
- Early evidence
- Measured performance

## About page

Structure:

1. What Rodney helps organizations do
2. Why broad experience matters
3. Relevant career proof
4. Healthcare and mission-driven experience
5. Approach to collaboration
6. Responsible use of technology
7. What oobCREATIVE is and is not
8. Start a Conversation

## Insights

Launch only with substantive first-hand material.

Editorial themes:

1. Making complex work understandable
2. Building communication capacity
3. Practical AI for human-led organizations

Do not create a high-volume generic blog.

## Start a Conversation

Purpose:

Confirm fit and boundaries without requiring visitors to diagnose the complete solution.

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Organization
- Email
- Which situation feels closest?
- What is happening now?
- What event or deadline matters?
- Who is involved in the decision?
- Approximate investment range, once approved
- Preferred next step

Also provide direct email.
