# Case Study System

## Purpose

Case studies are the primary proof system for the oobCREATIVE website, outreach, proposals, and sales conversations.

## Outcome labels

Use one or more of these labels:

- **Completed work:** Deliverables and systems are finished.
- **Strategic readiness:** The organization has a clearer position or platform, but market performance is not yet measured.
- **Early evidence:** Initial donations, sponsorships, adoption, or use are documented with appropriate attribution.
- **Measured performance:** Post-launch data supports a direct or carefully attributed result.

## Attribution rule

Separate:

- What oobCREATIVE completed
- What the combined client and partner effort produced
- What remains an opportunity or hypothesis

## Publication status

Use:

- `approved-internal`
- `client-permission-pending`
- `approved-public`
- `deprecated`
