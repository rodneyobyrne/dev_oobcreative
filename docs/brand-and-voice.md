\# oobCREATIVE Brand and Voice Guidelines



\## Purpose



oobCREATIVE helps organizations clarify complicated ideas, organize information, develop meaningful content, and turn that work into useful digital experiences.



The website should demonstrate strategic thinking, creative judgment, technical capability, and practical execution.



It should not present oobCREATIVE as a large agency or an automated website factory.



\## Brand Position



oobCREATIVE operates at the intersection of:



\- Brand strategy

\- Content development

\- Information architecture

\- Website design

\- Digital production

\- AI-assisted workflows

\- Visual and audio communication



The work combines human judgment with modern production tools.



AI may accelerate research, organization, development, and execution. It does not replace accountability, taste, context, or human decision-making.



\## Core Brand Qualities



The brand should feel:



\- Intelligent

\- Human

\- Curious

\- Direct

\- Resourceful

\- Unconventional

\- Trustworthy

\- Experienced

\- Practical



The brand should not feel:



\- Corporate

\- Inflated

\- Generic

\- Trend-driven

\- Overly polished

\- Artificially inspirational

\- Technically performative

\- Aggressively sales-focused



\## Audience



Primary audiences may include:



\- Small organizations

\- Nonprofits

\- Consultants

\- Behavioral health organizations

\- Community programs

\- Mission-driven businesses

\- Founders

\- Leaders managing complicated communication problems

\- Clients who need senior-level thinking without a large agency structure



The audience may understand their business well but struggle to organize, explain, present, or publish it clearly.



\## Value Proposition



oobCREATIVE helps clients move from:



\- Disconnected information to coherent structure

\- Rough ideas to usable content

\- Complicated messages to clear communication

\- Strategy to implementation

\- Stalled projects to forward movement

\- Traditional workflows to practical AI-assisted systems



Do not reduce the value proposition to inexpensive website production.



\## Voice



The voice should be plain, intelligent, observant, and human.



It should sound like an experienced person explaining something clearly—not a marketing department performing expertise.



Use confidence without exaggeration.



Use personality without becoming theatrical.



Use humor or unconventional phrasing only when it improves connection or clarity.



\## Point of View



The website may use first-person language when appropriate.



Keep the founder visible enough to establish accountability and experience, but do not make every page autobiographical.



Prefer:



> I help organizations make complicated ideas easier to understand and use.



Avoid:



> I am a visionary creative thought leader delivering transformative solutions.



The work and thinking should remain the primary focus.



\## Writing Style



Use:



\- Short, complete sentences

\- Direct statements

\- Natural rhythm

\- Clear transitions

\- Specific language

\- Human explanations

\- Useful examples

\- Strong headings

\- Occasional contrast

\- Purposeful white space



Avoid:



\- Long comma-heavy sentences

\- Repetitive sentence openings

\- Dense lists

\- Excessive three-part phrasing

\- Empty marketing claims

\- Unnecessary jargon

\- Buzzwords

\- Inflated promises

\- Generic agency language

\- Excessive use of “we”

\- Overexplaining simple ideas



\## Sentence Rhythm



Vary sentence length naturally.



Two connected ideas followed by a short statement often works well.



Example:



> The problem is rarely a lack of information. It is usually a lack of structure. That can be fixed.



Avoid repetitive constructions such as:



> A better website. A better message. A better strategy.



Avoid stacking several abstract nouns into one sentence.



\## Tone by Context



\### Homepage



Confident, distinct, and easy to understand.



The homepage should quickly explain:



\- What oobCREATIVE does

\- Who it helps

\- Why the approach is different

\- What the visitor should do next



\### Service Pages



Practical and outcome-focused.



Explain the problem, the approach, and the expected value without making unsupported promises.



\### About Page



Personal enough to establish trust.



Focus on relevant experience, perspective, and working style rather than a complete professional biography.



\### Case Studies



Specific and factual.



Show the situation, decisions, work completed, and observable outcomes.



Do not exaggerate results.



\### Articles and Insights



Curious, analytical, and useful.



Explain reasoning. Challenge assumptions where appropriate.



\## Content Principles



Every page should answer at least one meaningful visitor question.



Examples:



\- Can this person understand my problem?

\- Can they organize complicated information?

\- Can they turn strategy into something usable?

\- Can I trust them to finish?

\- Will the process be manageable?

\- Is this a good fit for my organization?



Content should be organized around visitor needs rather than internal company categories alone.



\## Content Guardrails



Do not invent:



\- Clients

\- Testimonials

\- Statistics

\- Revenue

\- Awards

\- Certifications

\- Partnerships

\- Outcomes

\- Project details

\- Years of experience

\- Technical capabilities

\- Team members



Do not publish confidential client information.



Do not present exploratory ideas as established services.



Do not describe AI as autonomous, infallible, or a replacement for human responsibility.



Do not use mental-health experiences as promotional material unless explicitly approved for a specific purpose.



\## AI Positioning



AI should be presented as part of a considered workflow.



Preferred framing:



\- AI-assisted

\- Human-directed

\- Faster iteration

\- Better organization

\- Expanded production capacity

\- Structured review

\- Clear approval

\- Responsible use



Avoid:



\- Fully automated

\- Effortless

\- Instant

\- Replace your team

\- No human involvement

\- One-click transformation



The message should be:



> Use AI where it improves the work. Keep humans responsible for the result.



\## Visual Direction



The visual system should feel distinctive without becoming chaotic.



Prefer:



\- Strong typography

\- Clear hierarchy

\- Generous spacing

\- Intentional asymmetry

\- Editorial structure

\- Unexpected but relevant imagery

\- Close-up environmental details

\- Real textures

\- Useful motion

\- Simple interaction patterns



Avoid:



\- Generic stock-office photography

\- Handshake imagery

\- Artificial team-meeting scenes

\- Excessive gradients

\- Decorative animation without purpose

\- Template-like agency layouts

\- Overcrowded sections

\- Visual trends that quickly date the site



\## Imagery



Images should communicate environment, context, detail, and emotional tone.



People do not always need to be the main subject.



Prefer:



\- Workspaces

\- Tools

\- Objects

\- Screens

\- Materials

\- Architecture

\- Hands performing meaningful work

\- Close-up details

\- Evidence of creative or technical process

\- Unusual visual relationships



Avoid images that merely illustrate the literal topic.



\## Calls to Action



Calls to action should be direct and low-pressure.



Possible language:



\- Start a conversation

\- Bring me the complicated part

\- Discuss the project

\- See how the work fits

\- Review the approach

\- Explore the services



Avoid:



\- Transform your business today

\- Unlock your potential

\- Supercharge your growth

\- Get started now

\- Book your breakthrough



\## Accessibility



Clarity is part of the brand.



The website should:



\- Use readable type sizes

\- Maintain sufficient contrast

\- Support keyboard navigation

\- Use clear labels

\- Use logical headings

\- Include meaningful alt text

\- Avoid unnecessary movement

\- Avoid text embedded inside important images

\- Avoid visual complexity that interferes with comprehension



\## SEO



Write for humans first.



Use clear page titles, headings, descriptions, and URLs.



Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.



Do not create thin pages solely to target search terms.



SEO content must remain accurate, useful, and consistent with the brand voice.



\## Working Principle



The website should show the value of thoughtful structure.



It should feel creative without becoming confusing.



It should feel experienced without becoming self-important.



It should use modern technology without allowing technology to become the message.



\## Authority



This document provides the default brand and voice direction for the website.



Specific instructions approved later by the user override this document.



When a requested change conflicts with these guidelines, identify the conflict before making the change.
