# Persona-to-Offer Matrix

| Persona | Stable identity | Immediate state | Protected value | Desired feeling | Primary public pathway | Likely service families | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frederick | Founder or owner | Everything still depends on him | Quality | Confident release | Help my team move without everything coming through me | Systems, website, ongoing support | Fewer approvals; documented standards; stronger team resources |
| Jonathan | Mission-driven executive | He repeatedly carries and translates the mission | Human impact | Shared responsibility | Help us explain the work clearly without losing its meaning | Messaging, storytelling, website, ongoing support | Audience pathways; reusable language; distributed storytelling |
| Brendan | Communications lead | Communication is reactive and structurally chaotic | Professional agency | Control and legitimacy | Help communication stop feeling chaotic | Systems, practical AI, ongoing support | Intake, approvals, priorities, reusable content |
| Devon | Proven expert | His value is difficult to understand or package | Intellectual depth | Accurately seen | Help people understand the value of what I do | Positioning, website, storytelling | Clear offer; framework; stronger decision pathway |
| Eli | AI adoption leader | Experimentation lacks direction and standards | Responsibility and trust | Informed confidence | Help us use AI where it genuinely improves the work | Practical AI, systems, implementation support | Prioritized use cases; tested workflows; review standards |
| Dave | Project-recovery leader | He cannot afford another opaque decision | Trust | Safety and control | Help me recover this project without repeating the same mistake | Depends on what failed | Salvage decision; phased plan; visible checkpoints |
| Doug | Emerging independent professional | He is preparing but not publicly committed | Possibility | Ready and credible | Help me turn this idea into something real enough to test | Positioning, launch, guided support | One audience; one offer; minimum viable presence; real test |

## Strong qualification signals

- The problem has a visible cost.
- The buyer has authority or a committed sponsor.
- The organization can make decisions.
- The buyer values quality and collaboration.
- The work fits communication, media, website, workflow, or practical AI capabilities.
- The buyer is willing to select priorities.
- A specific event, transition, failure, or growth condition creates urgency.

## Disqualification signals

- The buyer wants unlimited access or undefined support.
- The buyer expects AI to replace judgment or accountability.
- The buyer wants volume without clarity.
- Leadership will not participate in required decisions.
- The organization lacks budget but expects custom full-service execution.
- The requested outcome depends mainly on management consulting outside oobCREATIVE’s scope.
- The buyer wants guaranteed business results that communication work alone cannot ensure.

## Many-to-many rule

One persona can need several service families, and one service family can serve several personas.

The persona determines:

- Entry language
- Emotional validation
- Objections
- Proof
- Participation style

The service family determines:

- Scope
- Deliverables
- Delivery method
- Pricing
- Operational capacity
