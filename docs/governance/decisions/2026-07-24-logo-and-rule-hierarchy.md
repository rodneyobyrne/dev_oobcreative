# Decision: Logo and Structural Rule Hierarchy

## Logo decision

The global header and homepage navigation use the standalone oobCREATIVE mark. Complete horizontal or stacked lockups are reserved for formal identity moments.

- Header: `/branding/Mark.png`
- Dark footer: `/branding/oob_horiz-flat-dark.png`
- Formal light-background use: `/branding/oob_horiz-flat-lite.png`

## Structural-line decision

Long horizontal section dividers and vertical column separators use subtle hand-drawn SVG lines. CTA rectangles, buttons, and other formal controls remain geometrically straight.

## Rationale

This preserves a subtle interface identity while making the complete lockup feel more official when it appears. The contrast between hand-drawn structural rules and clean CTA geometry reinforces the brand without making the site look decorative or informal.
