# Decision: Increase Hand-Rule Weight

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Status:** Approved visual-system correction

## Decision

Increase the thickness and roughness of decorative horizontal and vertical hand-drawn rules so their visual weight better relates to the outline weight of the approved oobCREATIVE mark.

## Applies To

- section separators
- pathway dividers
- selected-work dividers
- comparable decorative structural rules

## Does Not Apply To

- navigation
- footer
- button borders
- CTA frames
- form controls
- fine UI outlines

Those remain unchanged and geometric.

## Implementation

The primary rough stroke is increased to approximately `8px`, supported by a secondary noisy stroke of approximately `4px`.

The clipped silhouette is intentionally more irregular than the previous version.
