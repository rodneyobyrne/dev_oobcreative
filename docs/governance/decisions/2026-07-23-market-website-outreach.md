# Market, Website, and Outreach Direction

## Decision

oobCREATIVE will be positioned as a human-led communications, website, and practical-AI implementation partner for mission-driven and expert-led organizations whose ideas, expertise, responsibilities, or growth have outgrown the systems supporting them.

## Working public position

> oobCREATIVE helps mission-driven and expert-led organizations turn complex work into clear messaging, useful websites, sustainable communication systems, and practical human-reviewed AI workflows.

## Why this direction

Rodney O'Byrne's experience is strongest when several disciplines must work together: audience insight, positioning, content, information architecture, design, websites, media, workflow, technology, and implementation.

The business will not market that range as a miscellaneous service list. It will use the range to solve one class of problem:

> Valuable work has become difficult to explain, manage, share, or move forward.

## Primary public markets

1. Behavioral health and human-service organizations
2. Nonprofits and community organizations
3. Expert-led firms and specialized consultants

## Primary buying roles

- Founder or executive leader
- Communications, development, or marketing leader
- Expert, clinician, or practice leader
- Operations or responsible-AI leader

## Internal persona use

The seven named personas remain internal buying-state archetypes. They guide emotional recognition, objections, proof, visual atmosphere, participation style, and calls to action.

They will not become seven public industries or seven equal top-level products.

## Public emotional conditions

The website will help visitors recognize five common conditions:

1. Our work is valuable but difficult to explain.
2. Too much still depends on one person.
3. Communication has become reactive and fragmented.
4. We need a practical and responsible way to use AI.
5. An important project has stalled or needs to be rebuilt.

Doug's early-stage condition will be served selectively through structured, fixed-scope work rather than shaping the primary website.

## Service structure

Three primary service families will lead the public website:

1. Positioning, Messaging and Websites
2. Communication Systems and Practical AI
3. Campaigns, Fundraising and Organizational Growth

Two secondary paths will be available:

- Project Recovery Assessment
- Ongoing Communications Partnership

## Website structure

The website will use a clear service and proof architecture while embedding emotional recognition within the homepage, audience pages, service pages, and case studies.

Primary navigation:

- Home
- Who We Help
- Services
- Work
- About
- Insights
- Start a Conversation

## Outreach structure

The first outreach system will use only:

1. Existing relationships
2. LinkedIn under Rodney O'Byrne's name
3. Strategic referral partners

Paid advertising, high-volume cold email, and daily content production are not part of the initial system.

## Proof priorities

The initial website will lead with:

- Rock and Roll Academy Aspen
- Everett Health Partners
- Varetto Recovery
- Mind Springs Health and West Springs Hospital

## Supersedes

This decision refines earlier working recommendations that treated four broad public audience groups and five equal service families as the default launch architecture.

The original persona and service research remains strategically useful. This decision establishes the simpler public system used for the first website build.
