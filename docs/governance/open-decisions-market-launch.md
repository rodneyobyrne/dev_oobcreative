# Market and Website Launch Decisions

These are the remaining decisions that should not be silently invented during website development.

## OD-L01 — Minimum engagement level

**Status:** Open  
**Blocking:** Blocks final service and contact-page publication; does not block page design.

Decide:

- Whether the website will state a minimum project investment
- Whether each service begins with a paid diagnostic or strategy phase
- Whether Guided engagements have a separate minimum

**Working recommendation:** Establish a paid first phase for custom work and a fixed price for Project Recovery Assessment.

## OD-L02 — Delivery capacity

**Status:** Open  
**Blocking:** Blocks promises about timelines and availability.

Decide:

- Number of simultaneous substantial engagements
- Work Rodney completes directly
- Work that requires collaborators
- Whether ongoing support is limited to prior clients

**Working recommendation:** Limit the business to two substantial active engagements plus one bounded Guided engagement until delivery data supports expansion.

## OD-L03 — Case-study publication permission

**Status:** Open by client  
**Blocking:** Blocks public publication of client names, screenshots, quotes, and sensitive details.

Confirm permission for:

- Rock and Roll Academy Aspen
- Everett Health Partners
- Varetto Recovery
- Mind Springs Health and West Springs Hospital

Internal drafts may be developed before permission is complete.

## OD-L04 — Public contact mechanism

**Status:** Open  
**Blocking:** Blocks the final Start a Conversation page.

Decide:

- Direct email
- Contact form
- Scheduling link
- Fit questionnaire
- Expected response time

**Working recommendation:** Use a short fit form plus direct email. Do not force scheduling before the visitor has explained the situation.

## OD-L05 — Insights launch threshold

**Status:** Open  
**Blocking:** Non-blocking.

Decide whether Insights launches with:

- Three complete articles
- Case-study expansions only
- A hidden route until sufficient material exists

**Working recommendation:** Launch Insights only after three substantial first-hand pieces are ready. Do not create an empty archive.

## OD-L06 — PayerKey public relationship

**Status:** Open  
**Blocking:** Blocks detailed public claims about PayerKey.

Confirm:

- Domain ownership
- Product and assessment ownership
- Everett Health Partners relationship
- Expert-validation responsibilities
- Permission to describe the product concept publicly

**Working recommendation:** Mention PayerKey only as a proposed product opportunity in the Everett case study until ownership and partnership terms are documented.
