# Imagery Guidelines

## Core approach

Environment is often more important than portraiture.

Images should communicate emotional condition through context, composition, working materials, light, friction, and movement. People may appear, but should not automatically become the focal point.

## Preferred characteristics

- Close or medium-close environmental crops
- Real working details
- Objects with narrative purpose
- Screens, papers, notes, tools, and spaces used meaningfully
- Evidence of process
- Competence under recognizable pressure
- Visual tension that remains controlled
- Texture and material specificity
- Human presence without staged lifestyle performance

## Avoid

- Generic meetings
- Handshakes
- People pointing at laptops
- Empty modern offices
- Stock-photo teamwork
- Head-in-hands overwhelm
- Heroic nonprofit imagery
- Influencer and stage-speaker imagery
- Robots or glowing AI interfaces
- Broken objects as project-recovery shorthand
- Aspirational luxury unrelated to the work

## Prompt structure

When generating or commissioning an image, specify:

1. Archetype and emotional temperature
2. Protected value
3. Desired movement
4. Environment
5. Meaningful objects
6. Composition and crop
7. Light and texture
8. What should not become the focal point
9. Clichés to avoid
10. Required aspect ratio and use

## Example prompt logic

```text
Create an editorial, photo-realistic website image for a capable communications lead experiencing contained irritation. Show competence constrained by layered requests and weak approvals. Focus on the environment: marked-up materials, an active calendar, repeated revisions, and a clear work surface. Use a close crop and neutral working light. Do not show comic chaos, head-in-hands burnout, or generic office teamwork.
```

## Asset rules

- Store website images in `public/images/`.
- Use lowercase descriptive filenames with hyphens.
- Preserve source files separately when needed.
- Record source, rights, status, intended use, and alt-text status in `asset-register.md`.
- Optimize delivery formats without discarding the original.
- Do not publish an image with unclear rights.
