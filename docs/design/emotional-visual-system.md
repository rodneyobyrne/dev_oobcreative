# Emotional and Visual Archetype System

## Purpose

This system translates internal persona knowledge into emotionally intelligent language, imagery, pacing, proof, and interaction.

It prevents two failures:

1. Treating every visitor with the same generic agency presentation
2. Building seven unrelated visual brands through ad hoc page styling

## Governing model

```text
One oobCREATIVE brand system
        +
Controlled emotional modulation
        =
Distinct recognition without brand fragmentation
```

## Brand constants

These should remain stable across archetypes:

- Logo and wordmark behavior
- Core typography system
- Accessibility standards
- Base layout logic
- Editorial clarity
- Image-quality standard
- Human-centered technology position
- Interaction quality
- Direct, low-pressure calls to action
- Respect for the visitor’s intelligence and agency

## Modulation controls

Archetype context may change:

- Emotional tension
- Composition density
- Amount of open space
- Image crop and viewpoint
- Environmental detail
- Light and contrast
- Pace and reveal
- Proof emphasis
- Amount of control shown
- CTA wording

These are controlled variables, not separate themes.

## Universal visual rules

- Show competence before distress.
- Show environment and friction rather than emotional caricature.
- Prefer close environmental details over generic portraits.
- Use people as participants in a system, not as decorative lifestyle subjects.
- Let objects, documents, tools, spaces, and sequences carry emotional meaning.
- Keep technology present only when relevant.
- Avoid literal clichés for chaos, leadership, mission, expertise, AI, failure, or ambition.

## Frederick — Controlled pressure

**Internal archetype:** Bottlenecked Founder  
**Protected value:** Quality  
**Desired feeling:** Confident release  
**Public recognition:** Too many decisions still return to you.

### Visual atmosphere

Competence compressed by responsibility. Quality review, late-day attention, recurring approvals, and too much flow toward one decision point.

### Composition

- Directional lines or materials converging toward one place
- Close crops of review, revision, or decision activity
- Controlled density rather than visible collapse
- A sense that the system is capable but overdependent

### Light and texture

- Restrained, mature light
- Late-day warmth or neutral working light
- Tactile paper, marked-up documents, screens, standards, and repeatable materials

### Interaction and pacing

- Show clear checkpoints
- Make transfer of judgment visible
- Use progressive disclosure rather than overwhelming dashboards

### Proof emphasis

- Documented standards
- Stronger first drafts
- Fewer approval loops
- Team resources
- Clearer decision pathways

### Avoid

- Luxury executive imagery
- “Let go and delegate” clichés
- Visible breakdown
- Technology replacing judgment

### CTA character

Confident and bounded: move the work without lowering the standard.

## Jonathan — Heavy responsibility

**Internal archetype:** Mission-Carrying Executive Director  
**Protected value:** Mission and human impact  
**Desired feeling:** Shared responsibility  
**Public recognition:** The work matters, but too much of its meaning depends on you translating it.

### Visual atmosphere

Human warmth with restrained seriousness. Multiple audiences, real consequences, and a mission carried through an organization rather than a heroic individual.

### Composition

- Environmental context suggesting staff, community, programs, funders, or families
- Multiple perspectives or pathways without visual chaos
- Weight and care without defeat
- Shared materials and distributed communication

### Light and texture

- Warm, natural, credible light
- Real community or organizational textures
- Avoid overly polished corporate surfaces

### Interaction and pacing

- Allow time for context
- Use clear audience pathways
- Make ethical review and shared resources visible

### Proof emphasis

- Reusable program language
- Audience-specific pathways
- Ethical story gathering
- Staff communication resources
- Reduced dependence on one leader

### Avoid

- Savior imagery
- Sentimental nonprofit clichés
- Exploitative participant portraits
- Corporate fundraising gloss

### CTA character

Supportive and responsible: help the organization carry the message together.

## Brendan — Contained irritation

**Internal archetype:** Overextended Communications Lead  
**Protected value:** Professional agency and communication quality  
**Desired feeling:** Control and legitimacy  
**Public recognition:** The issue is upstream. You are being asked to compensate for weak priorities and decisions.

### Visual atmosphere

Competence constrained by repeated interruption. Active inputs, layered requests, revisions, and competing priorities without making Brendan look disorganized.

### Composition

- Open tabs, marked-up materials, calendars, requests, and approval layers
- Structured visual friction
- Focused posture under pressure
- Evidence that the person is capable and the system is the problem

### Light and texture

- Neutral working light
- Moderate contrast
- Active but controlled density
- Repetition and layering used intentionally

### Interaction and pacing

- Clear intake and priority logic
- Visible decision rights
- Reduced repetition
- Fast recognition followed by calm structure

### Proof emphasis

- Intake and approval processes
- Priority rules
- Reusable content libraries
- Human-reviewed AI standards
- Fewer preventable revisions

### Avoid

- Chaotic-desk comedy
- Head-in-hands burnout imagery
- Productivity advice aesthetics
- “Content at scale” visuals

### CTA character

Direct and legitimizing: turn recurring chaos into a managed function.

## Devon — Intellectual frustration

**Internal archetype:** Expert With Unpackaged Value  
**Protected value:** Intellectual depth  
**Desired feeling:** Accurately seen  
**Public recognition:** The value is clear in conversation but difficult to understand from the outside.

### Visual atmosphere

Layered intelligence not yet organized into a public path. Depth, notes, frameworks, patterns, and material that contains more clarity than the current presentation reveals.

### Composition

- Books, notes, diagrams, working materials, and thoughtful detail
- Space for reflection combined with areas of dense information
- Close crops that imply an underlying system
- Movement from scattered insight toward a coherent framework

### Light and texture

- Thoughtful, editorial light
- Material depth and subtle contrast
- Avoid aspirational luxury

### Interaction and pacing

- Let the visitor explore nuance without losing orientation
- Reveal structure gradually
- Use precise labels and decision pathways

### Proof emphasis

- Defined audience and offer
- Articulated framework
- Clear service distinctions
- Reusable source material
- Better prospect self-selection

### Avoid

- Guru stages
- Influencer portraits
- “Package your genius” language
- Forced simplification

### CTA character

Accurate and respectful: make the depth understandable without flattening it.

## Eli — Alert caution

**Internal archetype:** Responsible AI Adopter  
**Protected value:** Responsibility, accuracy, and trust  
**Desired feeling:** Informed confidence  
**Public recognition:** The organization is experimenting, but responsibility and workflow are still unclear.

### Visual atmosphere

Evaluation, comparison, testing, and human review. Technology is present but subordinate to responsibility and real work.

### Composition

- Workflow cues, comparison, review, and decision points
- Human judgment in the foreground
- Clean active workspace rather than futuristic spectacle
- Clear separation between tool output and human approval

### Light and texture

- Clean neutral or slightly cool light
- High legibility
- Controlled technical detail
- No neon or science-fiction glow

### Interaction and pacing

- Show limits and permissions
- Use pilots, checkpoints, and review standards
- Make “what not to automate” visible

### Proof emphasis

- Prioritized use cases
- Tested workflows
- Human responsibility
- Review standards
- Staff guidance tied to real tasks

### Avoid

- Robots
- Glowing interfaces
- AI evangelism
- Speed and volume claims
- Tool-first presentation

### CTA character

Measured and testable: start with one useful, defensible workflow.

## Dave — Guarded distrust

**Internal archetype:** Rebuilding Leader  
**Protected value:** Trust  
**Desired feeling:** Safety and control  
**Public recognition:** You have already paid for progress once. The next decision must be visible and defensible.

### Visual atmosphere

Calm review, salvage, sequencing, and measured rebuilding. Existing assets are examined rather than dismissed.

### Composition

- Partially completed materials
- Annotated plans
- Before, salvage, repair, and next-step sequences
- Transparent layers and visible checkpoints
- Space that lowers pressure

### Light and texture

- Clear, honest, neutral light
- Moderate contrast
- Materials showing use, revision, and recoverable value

### Interaction and pacing

- Small steps
- Explicit choices
- Side-by-side options
- No hidden process
- Confirmation before commitment

### Proof emphasis

- Failure-point explanation
- Salvage-versus-rebuild decision
- Phased scope
- Checkpoints
- Recovered usable assets

### Avoid

- Broken glass
- Disaster imagery
- “Fresh start” hype
- Opaque proposal aesthetics
- Overconfidence

### CTA character

Safe and bounded: begin with the smallest useful review.

## Doug — Hopeful hesitation

**Internal archetype:** Quietly Ambitious Professional  
**Protected value:** Possibility  
**Desired feeling:** Ready and credible  
**Public recognition:** You do not need everything settled. You need one credible version you can test.

### Visual atmosphere

A threshold between private preparation and public participation. Notes becoming a plan. Modest forward energy with room to revise.

### Composition

- Beginnings, thresholds, first versions, and simple plans
- Open space with a few chosen elements
- Evidence of preparation becoming action
- Modest, credible work environments

### Light and texture

- Lighter, open, natural light
- Optimism without exaggerated brightness
- Simple tactile materials

### Interaction and pacing

- Reversible choices
- One audience, one offer, one test
- Clear next decision
- Avoid presenting a complete identity system too early

### Proof emphasis

- Minimum viable presence
- One testable offer
- One real outreach or launch test
- Defined next decision

### Avoid

- Startup-founder clichés
- Luxury aspiration
- “Build your empire” language
- Overbuilt brand systems

### CTA character

Encouraging and constrained: create a credible first version and put it in front of someone.

## Cross-archetype review questions

Before approving a page or image, confirm:

- Does the visitor appear competent?
- Is the source of friction represented respectfully?
- Is the protected value visible?
- Does the composition support the desired movement?
- Is the emotional temperature distinct without leaving the brand system?
- Is proof matched to the archetype’s trust requirement?
- Does the CTA preserve agency?
- Have literal clichés been avoided?
