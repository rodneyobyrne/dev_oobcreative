# Forms and Contact System

## Status

No production contact form destination is approved.

Do not implement a live form, scheduler, automation, or data collection until the following decisions are made.

## Required decisions

- Direct email, form, scheduler, or combination
- Form processor or destination
- Required fields
- Optional qualification questions
- Spam protection
- Success and error behavior
- Expected response time
- Privacy language
- Data retention
- Accessibility

## Emotional requirement

The contact experience should confirm a bounded next step.

It should not ask the visitor to explain everything before understanding what oobCREATIVE may offer.

Possible flow:

1. Identify the situation or project type
2. Identify the desired movement
3. Select a participation preference when relevant
4. Provide enough context to confirm fit
5. Explain what happens after submission

## Data minimization

Collect only information needed to respond and qualify the request.

The privacy page must accurately describe the final tools and handling.
