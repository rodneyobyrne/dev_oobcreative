# Knowledge System Glossary

**Archetype**  
An internal buying-state model used to understand emotional state, protected value, objections, proof needs, and desired movement. Archetype names are not normally public labels.

**Canonical document**  
The one authoritative editable document for a subject.

**Source archive**  
Unchanged original material preserved for traceability. It is not edited as strategy evolves.

**Working**  
Organized and useful, but not authorized as final direction.

**Approved**  
Explicitly accepted and authorized to guide implementation.

**Published**  
Currently represented on the live website.

**Deprecated**  
Retained for history but no longer authoritative.

**Emotional state**  
The immediate pressure or condition the visitor recognizes.

**Protected value**  
What the visitor fears losing while changing, such as quality, trust, depth, mission, agency, or possibility.

**Desired movement**  
The practical and emotional shift the work should create.

**Public pathway**  
A recognizable situation expressed in visitor language that connects an emotional state to relevant help.

**Service family**  
A descriptive public category defining the type of work oobCREATIVE may provide.

**Participation mode**  
How responsibility is shared: Guided, Co-Created, or Full Implementation.

**Page brief**  
The approved contract translating strategy into one page’s purpose, content, visual atmosphere, proof, choices, and CTA.

**Proof**  
Verifiable evidence such as an approved project, result, testimonial, credential, screenshot, or professional-history fact.

**Brand constant**  
A visual or verbal rule that remains stable across the entire website.

**Persona modulation**  
A controlled adjustment to emotional temperature, composition, imagery, pacing, proof, or CTA while preserving the shared brand.

**Blocking decision**  
An unresolved question that would force implementation to make an unjustified strategic assumption.
