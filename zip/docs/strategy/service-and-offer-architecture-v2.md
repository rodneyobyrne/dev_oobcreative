# Service and Offer Architecture v2

## Public service system

The launch website will lead with three primary service families. Two secondary paths support recovery and continuation.

## 1. Positioning, Messaging and Websites

### Starting condition

The work is valuable, but the organization, service, program, or expertise is difficult to understand or choose.

### Movement

From scattered explanation to a clear market, audience, message, and digital decision experience.

### Possible work

- Audience and buyer research
- Positioning and differentiation
- Message architecture
- Brand clarification
- Service and program organization
- Website information architecture
- Website content
- Visual direction
- Website design and implementation
- Search and AI-discovery foundations

### Early visible win

A concise audience, problem, and positioning statement that aligns the project before full production.

### Completion evidence

- Defined audience pathways
- Clear service or program structure
- Approved message architecture
- Working website or implementation-ready specification
- Stronger prospect self-selection
- Reusable source language

### Primary proof

- Everett Health Partners
- Varetto Recovery
- Rock and Roll Academy Aspen

## 2. Communication Systems and Practical AI

### Starting condition

Communication is repetitive, reactive, scattered, dependent on memory, or burdened by unclear approvals. AI experimentation may be adding speed without adding control.

### Movement

From repeated reconstruction and informal workarounds to reusable knowledge, clear decisions, and human-reviewed workflows.

### Possible work

- Communication workflow review
- Request intake and prioritization
- Approval roles and timelines
- Reusable messaging and content libraries
- Grant and campaign source systems
- AI use-case selection
- Prompt and source structures
- Human-review standards
- Lightweight automation
- Team implementation support

### Early visible win

One high-friction recurring task is mapped, simplified, and tested.

### Completion evidence

- Defined intake and approval process
- Reusable source library
- Named human and AI responsibilities
- Tested workflows
- Review standards
- Fewer preventable revisions or repeated searches

### Primary proof

- Rock and Roll Academy grant library
- Behavioral-health education workflow
- Everett and PayerKey architecture
- Agency workflow and profitability improvements

## 3. Campaigns, Fundraising and Organizational Growth

### Starting condition

The organization is launching, expanding, fundraising, recruiting, or trying to build public understanding without one connected campaign system.

### Movement

From isolated promotional requests to a coordinated story, audience pathway, asset system, and rollout plan.

### Possible work

- Campaign positioning
- Fundraising narratives
- Sponsorship communication
- Program and service launches
- Media relations
- Email and social rollout
- Event promotion
- Human-centered storytelling
- Donor and audience pathways
- Visual and media production
- Internal campaign coordination

### Early visible win

A campaign brief aligns the audience, message, action, proof, assets, and rollout sequence.

### Completion evidence

- Approved campaign narrative
- Connected landing and response pathways
- Reusable asset system
- Defined distribution plan
- Sponsor, donor, family, or public messaging
- Launch-ready materials

### Primary proof

- Building the Backline
- Mind Springs communication campaigns
- Aspen Strong
- Varetto Recovery launch

## Secondary path: Project Recovery Assessment

### Purpose

A paid, bounded first phase for a failed, stalled, or disappointing website, campaign, brand, media, or technology project.

### Outputs

- Existing asset and decision review
- Failure-point analysis
- Salvage-versus-rebuild recommendation
- Priority and scope definition
- Phased recovery plan
- First repair recommendation

### Guardrail

Do not begin with a large rebuild proposal. Make the situation visible before asking for a larger commitment.

## Secondary path: Ongoing Communications Partnership

### Purpose

Continuing senior guidance and implementation after a defined initial engagement.

### Possible work

- Monthly priorities
- Website and content updates
- Campaign support
- Message review
- Visual and media direction
- Workflow refinement
- AI-system improvement
- Vendor coordination

### Guardrail

This is not unlimited access. Define monthly capacity, response expectations, included work, and rollover rules.

## Participation modes

### Guided

The client completes more of the work using structure, templates, review, and accountability.

### Co-Created

oobCREATIVE and the client develop the thinking and materials together.

### Full Implementation

oobCREATIVE leads strategy, creation, coordination, and implementation within explicit boundaries.

Participation modes do not replace service categories. They explain how the work is shared.
