# Recovery Looks Different

## Turning Varetto Recovery's philosophy into a clear, credible, and practical digital experience

Varetto Recovery was preparing to open Alan's House, a recovery residence for men in Des Moines, Iowa.

The organization believed recovery should be personal, practical, and grounded in progress. Residents should have structure, accountability, compassion, and community without being forced into one narrow definition of success.

The challenge was making that philosophy understandable and trustworthy.

## The communication problem

Recovery housing is rarely selected through a simple consumer decision.

A prospective resident may be balancing employment, family expectations, financial pressure, shame, legal concerns, and fear of losing independence. A parent, partner, therapist, employer, attorney, case manager, or peer may begin the search.

The website therefore needed to communicate with:

- The man considering Alan's House
- The person helping him evaluate or act on the decision

Varetto also supported harm reduction, self-directed recovery, medication-assisted treatment, accountability, and the recognition that progress may look different for each resident.

The central question became:

> How can a different approach to recovery feel understandable, responsible, and safe?

## Audience and search research

The engagement began with research into:

- Why men delay seeking help
- What moves them toward recovery housing
- Who influences the decision
- Which practical fears prevent action
- What families and referral sources need before contact

Four resident profiles were developed:

- The Young Achiever
- The Working-Class Dad
- The Veteran or First Responder
- The Professional Under Pressure

Influencer profiles were developed alongside each resident type.

Search strategy included:

- Program searches
- Decision-stage questions
- Influencer questions

The research shaped page order, FAQ content, admissions pathways, and calls to action—not only metadata.

## Brand clarification

Varetto already had a mission, founder story, values, recovery philosophy, and the Alan's House residence model.

The work organized those elements around one central truth:

> Recovery looks different for every man.

The voice became:

- Compassionate without becoming sentimental
- Direct without becoming punitive
- Personal without losing credibility
- Distinct without attacking other models
- Hopeful without promising outcomes

## Brand architecture

The website clarified:

- Varetto Recovery as the organization and broader vision
- Alan's House as the specific recovery residence

This gave Alan's House a clear local identity while allowing Varetto to support additional homes, services, or future programs.

## Website and content system

### Homepage

Introduced individualized recovery supported by structure, accountability, and connection.

### Alan's House

Explained the Drake Neighborhood, transportation, parking, bedrooms, shared spaces, home gym, nearby amenities, household standards, and community life.

### About

Connected the philosophy to lived experience and behavioral-health knowledge while preserving appropriate personal and professional boundaries.

### Admissions

Clarified the path from inquiry through application, conversation, screening, and move-in consideration. It recognized that another person may begin the inquiry while requiring the prospective resident to participate.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Organized operational answers about:

- Medication-assisted treatment
- Employment
- Transportation
- Visitors and children
- Prohibited items
- Daily routines
- Meetings and recovery participation
- Staffing and governance
- Rent and deposits
- Roommates
- Laundry and household needs
- Alumni involvement

### Trust and policy guidance

Reviewed privacy, terms, non-discrimination, accessibility, emergency guidance, disclaimers, copyright, and contact information.

The site avoided implying licensing or accreditation that did not exist.

## Visual and user-experience work

- Logo presentation
- Typography and color consistency
- Spacing and hierarchy
- Reduced text density
- Clear distinctions among philosophy, residence information, and admissions
- Stronger calls to action
- Desktop and mobile refinement

The intended visual position was warm but not soft, personal but not informal, structured but not institutional, and hopeful without becoming idealized.

## Verified outcomes

Post-launch traffic, inquiries, occupancy, and conversion data were not available.

Observable outcomes include:

- Distinct positioning around individualized recovery with structure and accountability
- Clear separation of Varetto Recovery and Alan's House
- Reduced decision friction through practical admissions and FAQ content
- Stronger trust through visible operations, expectations, leadership, and limits
- A scalable parent-brand architecture
- A completed public website

## Larger result

The website translated a deeply personal and unconventional recovery philosophy into a responsible public promise.

Prospective residents were not asked to surrender individuality to receive structure. Families and referral sources were not expected to make a high-stakes decision without practical information.

> Recovery could look different without looking uncertain.
