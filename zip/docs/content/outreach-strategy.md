# Simple and Sustainable Outreach Strategy

## Objective

Create a steady flow of qualified conversations without building a high-volume content or cold-outreach machine.

## Initial channels

Use only:

1. Existing relationships
2. LinkedIn under Rodney O'Byrne's name
3. Strategic referral partners

Do not begin with paid advertising, daily social posting, mass cold email, or a complicated funnel.

## Weekly rhythm

Approximate time: three hours.

### One useful LinkedIn post

Use a real lesson from completed work.

Structure:

1. One recognizable situation
2. One strategic observation
3. One practical implication
4. One relevant case-study link

Examples:

- Rock and Roll Academy did not have an impact problem. It had a structure-behind-the-impact problem.
- A consulting team can have exceptional credentials and still look interchangeable.
- Recovery-housing websites must communicate with the resident and the person helping him decide.
- A grant library is not just a writing resource. It reduces organizational dependence on memory.
- AI does not repair a broken approval process.

### Five personal messages

Send individual messages to:

- Former clients
- Former colleagues
- Community leaders
- Healthcare contacts
- Nonprofit executives
- Consultants
- Agency contacts
- People who have referred work

Do not disguise a broadcast message as personal outreach.

### Two follow-ups or introduction requests

Follow up with people who responded, engaged with a post, or may know an appropriate organization.

### Fifteen-minute pipeline review

Track:

- Person
- Organization
- Sector
- Relevant situation
- Last contact
- Next action
- Referral possibility
- Case study shared
- Conversation status

A spreadsheet is sufficient.

## Monthly rhythm

Each month:

- Publish one substantial case study or first-hand insight
- Send one brief relationship email
- Hold two referral-partner conversations
- Add five qualified organizations to a target list
- Ask one client or collaborator for a testimonial, metric, or introduction
- Review which message produced actual conversations

## Relationship email

**Subject:** A clearer focus for oobCREATIVE

> I have been consolidating the work oobCREATIVE does best: helping behavioral-health, mission-driven, and expert-led organizations clarify complicated work and build the communication systems around it.
>
> Recent projects have included a regional nonprofit fundraising system, a buyer-led behavioral-health consultancy website, and the launch of a men's recovery residence.
>
> I am looking for organizations where the work is valuable but difficult to explain, communication depends too heavily on one person, or an important initiative needs clearer structure and implementation.
>
> If someone comes to mind through your network, I would appreciate an introduction. Here is a recent example of the work: [relevant case study]

## Trigger-based outreach

> I saw that your organization is expanding into [program, location, service, campaign, or leadership transition].
>
> I work with organizations at the point where growth creates a communication problem: the work is strong, but the website, messaging, fundraising materials, or internal systems have not caught up.
>
> I recently helped [relevant organization] address a related transition. The circumstances are different, but the approach may be useful. Here is the case study: [link]

## Referral-partner outreach

> I am focusing oobCREATIVE on behavioral-health, nonprofit, and expert-led organizations that need messaging, websites, communication systems, campaigns, or practical AI implementation.
>
> Your work often puts you close to organizations encountering those problems. I would like to understand what you see before clients reach the point of hiring someone like me and identify where a referral relationship would be useful.

## Priority referral partners

- Grant writers
- Nonprofit consultants
- Fractional CFOs
- Healthcare and behavioral-health consultants
- Compliance and payer specialists
- Executive coaches
- CRM and technology implementers
- Web developers who do not provide strategy
- Fundraising professionals
- Community foundations
- Professional associations
- Public-relations specialists
- Attorneys serving healthcare or nonprofits

## Ninety-day operating plan

### Days 1–30 — Sales foundation

- Complete the revised website content architecture
- Prepare the four flagship case studies
- Update LinkedIn headline and About section
- Create a simple outreach tracker
- Confirm case-study permissions
- Set the minimum engagement and delivery capacity

### Days 31–60 — Relationship activation

- Send 20 personal reconnection messages
- Hold eight referral-partner conversations
- Publish four LinkedIn posts
- Publish one substantial insight
- Request three testimonials, metrics, or introductions
- Prepare one concise capabilities PDF

### Days 61–90 — Qualified pipeline

- Select 20 target organizations
- Identify a visible trigger for each
- Send 10 highly relevant messages
- Continue five relationship messages each week
- Publish four additional posts
- Publish one new case-study expansion or insight
- Review which audience, message, and proof generate conversations

## Operating targets

By day 90:

- 60 meaningful personal contacts
- 12 referral-partner conversations
- 8–12 LinkedIn insight posts
- 4 substantial proof assets
- 8 qualified introductory conversations
- 3 scoped opportunities
- 1–2 new engagements

These are operating targets, not guarantees.

## Sustainability rules

- Prefer one strong post over several generic posts.
- Reuse case-study research across LinkedIn, email, proposals, and the website.
- Do not create content without a real observation or proof source.
- Stop channels that consume time without producing qualified conversations.
- Record objections and questions; use them to improve service pages and outreach.
- Protect delivery time. Outreach should support the work rather than become a second full-time job.
