# Visual Component Guidance

## Header

- Use the complete approved logo lockup.
- Keep navigation sparse and horizontally aligned on larger screens.
- Use a single clear action: `Start a Conversation`.
- Avoid heavy backgrounds, oversized controls, or decorative icons.

## Hero

Default hero:

- Mostly typographic
- Large headline
- Tone-on-tone word emphasis
- Generous open space
- One primary action
- Optional sparse hand-drawn line

Photography variant:

- Use only when the intended persona benefits from lifestyle or process texture.
- Use one close-up action photograph.
- Keep the text and photograph visually separate.
- Use a partial hand-drawn frame or drawn line as a restrained connector.

## Recognition pathways

- Present no more than three primary paths in the first decision area.
- Use typography and spacing before icons.
- If marks are used, use simple unique doodles rather than repeated box symbols.
- Keep descriptions short and emotionally recognizable.

## Service and audience sections

- Prefer open rows, columns, and rules over cards.
- Use cards only when the content requires a contained comparison.
- Avoid large collections of outlined rectangles.

## Case studies

- Lead with the strategic problem and verified result.
- Use one image per featured case when available.
- Close-up process photography is preferred over generic client imagery.
- A text-only case-study presentation is acceptable and often preferred.

## Calls to action

- Use one strong action per section.
- Buttons may use clean geometric outlines.
- A hand-drawn underline, corner, or line may support the action.
- Do not frame every CTA with a doodle.

## Footer

- Keep compact and high contrast.
- Repeat the complete logo lockup only if it remains visually clear at the required size.
- Include essential navigation and legal links without decorative excess.

## Responsive behavior

- Preserve whitespace on mobile rather than compressing every section.
- Stack content in a clear reading order.
- Do not shrink hand-drawn marks until they become visually noisy.
- Remove nonessential decorative marks at small sizes.
