# oobCREATIVE Visual Design System

## Core principle

oobCREATIVE provides the calm, clear visual structure. Persona-specific content supplies the emotional atmosphere.

> Minimal brand. Emotionally specific content.

The brand should remain visually consistent even when the content speaks to different emotional states.

## Brand character

The oobCREATIVE visual system should feel:

- Clean
- Familiar
- Confident
- Structured
- Refreshing
- New
- Spacious
- Human without becoming sentimental
- Creative without becoming chaotic
- Technically capable without looking like a technology company

## Default visual hierarchy

Use this order of importance:

1. Typography
2. Space
3. Structure
4. Sparse hand-drawn line accents
5. Photography only when justified by the persona or content

Photography is not the default visual foundation.

## Color system

Use a restrained monochrome system:

- Near black
- White
- A small neutral-gray range
- Tone-on-tone shifts for emphasis

Do not assign separate branded color palettes to personas. Emotional differences should come primarily from language, imagery, pacing, composition, and density.

## Typography

Use a clean sans-serif foundation.

The `oobCREATIVE` word treatment establishes a recurring typographic behavior:

- Tone-on-tone color shift
- Contrasting font weights
- Strong hierarchy without decorative display typography
- Large headings with generous surrounding space

Tone and weight shifts may emphasize selected words in headings, but should remain restrained and readable.

Avoid:

- Decorative typefaces as the primary voice
- Dense all-caps text
- Excessive font families
- Repetitive slogan-like cadence
- Typography used merely to fill space

## Layout

Default layout qualities:

- Generous outer margins
- Wide breathing room between sections
- Few elements visible at one time
- Strong hierarchy
- Mostly one- and two-column structures
- Controlled line length
- Large visual pauses
- Minimal borders, cards, panels, and shadows
- Clear mobile stacking

Roominess is functional. It should help complicated information feel manageable.

## Hand-drawn elements

The hand-drawn quality of the logo may be echoed through sparse secondary marks.

Approved uses:

- Irregular horizontal rules
- Partial frames
- Underlines
- Directional arrows
- Small emphasis marks
- Section dividers
- Restrained corner details

Rules:

- Use marks sparingly.
- Maintain natural irregularity.
- Keep marks visually secondary to the content.
- Use marks to clarify structure or movement, not as filler.
- Hide purely decorative marks from assistive technology.

Do not:

- Repeat the logo box as an icon system
- Redraw or reinterpret the box
- Scatter open-box illustrations across the site
- Use a doodle in every section
- Allow line accents to create visual noise

## Photography

The default page should work without photography.

When the persona or content benefits from human or lifestyle texture, photography may be introduced as a controlled variant.

Photography should:

- Appear one meaningful image at a time
- Show close-up action, process, or working detail
- Avoid faces and full-body figures by default
- Use black-and-white or restrained neutral toning
- Use tight, deliberate crops
- Remain subordinate to typography and structure
- Be framed or accompanied by a sparse hand-drawn line only when useful

## Persona modulation

Personas may influence:

- Copy tone
- Image subject
- Light and shadow
- Cropping
- Visual tension
- Information density
- Section pacing
- Proof presentation
- CTA language

Personas must not change:

- Logo treatment
- Core typography
- Base palette
- Navigation behavior
- Fundamental grid
- Button system
- Overall brand identity

## Motion

Use restrained motion only when it clarifies progression or reinforces the hand-drawn system.

Approved examples:

- A line drawing itself once
- A partial frame completing on reveal
- A subtle tonal transition
- Content appearing with controlled spacing

Avoid:

- Floating decorative objects
- Large parallax effects
- Continuous looping animation
- Glowing technology effects
- Motion that delays access to content

## Accessibility

- Maintain WCAG-compliant contrast.
- Ensure keyboard focus is clearly visible.
- Do not use tonal differences as the only means of communicating meaning.
- Do not place essential text inside images.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep decorative doodles hidden from screen readers.
